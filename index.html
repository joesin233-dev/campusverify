About this tool

What it is

CampusVerify is an independent verification assistant. For each licensed institution, it crawls their own already-public pages live and compares numbers, accounts, and messages against what's actually published — nothing more.

What it isn't

  • Not an official system of, or partnership with, any listed institution.
  • Not a guarantee that every scam will be caught.
  • Not connected to any institution's private student records or systems.

Where the information comes from

Every contact and account shown is crawled live from that institution's own approved public pages at the moment you check — with the source page and check time shown next to it.

How institutions get added

Only institutions on CampusVerify's licensed/pilot list can be checked. This keeps every result tied to pages we've verified are genuinely official, rather than opening the tool to any link.

If anything here looks outdated, always confirm directly with your institution using the contacts on their official website.
